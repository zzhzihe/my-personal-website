Course report · Data-driven health insights

Weather-related Disease Prediction: Data-driven Health Insights

Prepared a Kaggle dataset combining weather variables, anonymized demographics, and more than 50 symptoms and medical-history factors. Used visualization, chi-square tests, ANOVA, and logistic regression to analyze relationships between weather and disease. The analysis found significant effects (p<0.0001); the heart-disease model reached a c-statistic of approximately 0.76 and identified patterns in age, temperature, humidity, and wind speed.

My role: Organized and analyzed the dataset, using multiple methods to examine relationships among variables.

Tools: SAS, Python, R
